Egress

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Egress ("the app") for Android handles. The app is published by LyfEscapes LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

We don't ask for your name or email, there are no accounts, and we don't run any servers that receive your data. Your progress, scores, purchases and settings are saved only on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play. Those Google services collect some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your current run, best score, owned items and chosen skin in the app's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded and interstitial ads. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • interactions with the app and ads (for example app launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Android settings. Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (Remove ads, skins, bundles) are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details. The app only learns which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

Egress is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold personal data on our own systems, there's nothing for us to access or delete on request. Ad-related data is controlled by Google; you can manage it through your Android ad settings and Google's privacy tools. If you have questions, contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
